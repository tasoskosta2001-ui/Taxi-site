Plan your Larnaca Airport transfer

The starting fares above are for one journey in a vehicle for 1–4 passengers during the day. Open your route to compare all four fares: day and night, for 1–4 or 1–6 passengers. Day rates apply from 06:00 to 22:00 and night rates from 22:00 to 06:00, using Cyprus time.

Arriving at Larnaca Airport

Include your flight number, arrival date, requested pickup time and full destination address in the booking notes. Tell us how many passengers and bags are travelling so we can confirm the suitable vehicle. The meeting point and availability are confirmed when we contact you.

Travelling to the airport

For a departure, give us the exact hotel or pickup address and the pickup time you need. Select the reverse direction in the airport booking form. Allow for your airline's check-in requirements when planning your journey.

Is sending the form a confirmed reservation?

No. Send your request by WhatsApp or email after checking the fare. Your reservation is confirmed only after we contact you. No online payment is required by the booking form.
