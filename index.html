Private taxi transfers across Cyprus

Welcome to Cyprus Taxi 24/7, a private taxi transfer service for airport and intercity journeys across Cyprus. We provide pre-booked transfers for individuals, families and groups of up to six passengers, with service available day and night. You can select your route, vehicle size, pickup date and exact time directly on this page and see the applicable fixed price before sending your booking.

Our main service areas include Larnaca Airport, Paphos Airport, Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, Ayia Napa and the Paralimni / Protaras area. Popular journeys include Larnaca Airport to Nicosia, Larnaca Airport to Limassol, Larnaca Airport to Ayia Napa, Paphos Airport to Limassol and private intercity transfers between the major cities and resorts. Reverse journeys are supported automatically, so the same fixed route prices apply when travelling back in the opposite direction.

Choose a vehicle for 1–4 passengers or a larger vehicle for 1–6 passengers. Day and night prices are clearly displayed, and once you choose the exact pickup time the correct period is selected automatically. Airport passengers can add their flight number, hotel or other pickup information before sending the completed request through WhatsApp. Our aim is a simple booking process, clear pricing and reliable private transport across Cyprus 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
