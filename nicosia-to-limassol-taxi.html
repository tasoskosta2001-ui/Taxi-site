Planning your Nicosia to Limassol journey

Tell us your pickup address in Nicosia and the exact destination in Limassol, such as your hotel, home or meeting address. This page is for a city-to-city journey; if your trip begins at an airport, choose the airport route instead.

Can I book Limassol to Nicosia?

Yes. Choose the reverse journey in the booking form. The same route fares apply in either direction. A return journey is a separate trip.

What does the starting fare cover?

The €95 starting fare is for the whole vehicle for 1–4 passengers during the day, from 06:00 to 22:00. Choose your vehicle and exact pickup time to see the applicable fare. Night fares apply from 22:00 to 06:00, using Cyprus time.

How do I confirm my transfer?

Enter the date, time, passenger count and pickup details, then send your request by WhatsApp or email. Mention luggage or other requirements in the notes. Your reservation is confirmed only after we contact you.
