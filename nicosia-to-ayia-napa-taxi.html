Private intercity transfer

Cyprus Taxi 24/7 provides direct door-to-door transfers between Nicosia and Ayia Napa. Choose a vehicle for 1–4 or 1–6 passengers, select your pickup date and exact time, and see the fixed route price before sending the booking. Day and night service is available seven days a week.

Planning your Nicosia to Ayia Napa journey

For a hotel or holiday stay in Ayia Napa, include the property name and address with your pickup details in Nicosia. If you are travelling to Protaras or Paralimni, select that destination in the booking form because it has a different fare.

Can I book Ayia Napa to Nicosia?

Yes. Choose the reverse journey in the booking form. The same route fares apply in either direction. A return journey is a separate trip.

What does the starting fare cover?

The €80 starting fare is for the whole vehicle for 1–4 passengers during the day, from 06:00 to 22:00. Choose your vehicle and exact pickup time to see the applicable fare. Night fares apply from 22:00 to 06:00, using Cyprus time.

How do I confirm my transfer?

Enter the date, time, passenger count and pickup details, then send your request by WhatsApp or email. Mention luggage or other requirements in the notes. Your reservation is confirmed only after we contact you.
