Planning your Limassol to Paphos journey

This fare covers a journey between Limassol and Paphos city. Paphos Airport is a separate destination: select the airport in the booking form if you are catching a flight. Include the exact pickup and drop-off addresses so we can confirm the trip details.

Can I book Paphos to Limassol?

Yes. Choose the reverse journey in the booking form. The same route fares apply in either direction. A return journey is a separate trip.

What does the starting fare cover?

The €75 starting fare is for the whole vehicle for 1–4 passengers during the day, from 06:00 to 22:00. Choose your vehicle and exact pickup time to see the applicable fare. Night fares apply from 22:00 to 06:00, using Cyprus time.

How do I confirm my transfer?

Enter the date, time, passenger count and pickup details, then send your request by WhatsApp or email. Mention luggage or other requirements in the notes. Your reservation is confirmed only after we contact you.
