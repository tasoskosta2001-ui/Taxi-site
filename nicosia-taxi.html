Before you travel

Can I book the reverse direction?

Yes. Each route above has separate links for travel from and to Nicosia. The listed starting price is for one journey, not a return trip.

Is the price per person?

The standard intercity fare is for the whole vehicle. Choose your vehicle and exact pickup time in the booking form to see the day or night fare.

How do student offers work?

Choose “Request a student offer” when entering your journey. Tell us if you need regular trips. Any offer and its conditions are confirmed before booking; the displayed standard fare is not automatically discounted.

Are other routes available?

Yes. All airport and intercity routes remain available in the main booking form.
