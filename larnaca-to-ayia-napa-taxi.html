Planning your Larnaca to Ayia Napa journey

Choose this route when your pickup is in Larnaca city. If you are arriving by plane, use the Larnaca Airport to Ayia Napa page instead and provide your flight number. For a hotel transfer, include the hotel name and full address in Ayia Napa.

Can I book Ayia Napa to Larnaca?

Yes. Choose the reverse journey in the booking form. The same route fares apply in either direction. A return journey is a separate trip.

What does the starting fare cover?

The €65 starting fare is for the whole vehicle for 1–4 passengers during the day, from 06:00 to 22:00. Choose your vehicle and exact pickup time to see the applicable fare. Night fares apply from 22:00 to 06:00, using Cyprus time.

How do I confirm my transfer?

Enter the date, time, passenger count and pickup details, then send your request by WhatsApp or email. Mention luggage or other requirements in the notes. Your reservation is confirmed only after we contact you.
